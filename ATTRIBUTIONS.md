This document logs all open-source, free-licensed, or public domain assets used within **Z-Gym Helper**.

## 3. Visual References
- Design system inspired by modern fitness web aesthetics (energetic acid lime, ink black, vibrant accents, and clean typography).
- Visual references from moodboards (Mura & Revamp) used exclusively for architectural layout, spacing rhythm, and visual balance. No proprietary logos, images, copy, or trainer assets from the reference images are replicated.

---

## 4. Exercise Imagery & Videos
- Exercise demonstrations: sourced from the public domain dataset [`free-exercise-db`](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) and verified open-licensed imagery.
- Video embeds: Rendered using privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` façades without scraping, downloading, or re-hosting video streams.

---

## 5. Nutrition Data
- Bangladeshi food nutritional compositions: Open data from Bangladesh Institute of Research and Rehabilitation in Diabetes, Endocrine and Metabolic Disorders (BIRDEM) nutritional tables, USDA FoodData Central, and Open Food Facts under Open Database License (ODbL).
